CAMPERVAN RULES IN SCOTLAND
--------------------------------------------------

Create an important information section.

Title:

Can You Park a Campervan Anywhere in Scotland?

Explain clearly:

No.

Scotland's famous "right to roam" does not mean campervans can park or camp anywhere.

Scottish outdoor access rights generally apply to responsible non-motorised recreation.

They do not give motor vehicles the right to drive onto or stay on private land.

When travelling with a campervan:

DO:
✓ use campsites
✓ use official motorhome stopovers
✓ use legal overnight parking
✓ use Forestry and Land Scotland Stay the Night locations
✓ respect local signs
✓ use designated campervan permit areas
✓ ask landowner permission where appropriate

DO NOT:
✕ park in passing places
✕ block gates
✕ drive onto grassland
✕ empty grey water into nature
✕ dump chemical toilet waste
✕ leave rubbish
✕ create a campsite on roadside parking areas

Explain:

When using simple overnight parking, behave as if you are parking rather than establishing a full campsite.

Keep chairs, tables, awnings and other equipment inside unless explicitly allowed.

Official Scottish Outdoor Access information:
https://www.outdooraccess-scotland.scot/

--------------------------------------------------
SINGLE-TRACK ROAD GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------

Add a small guide specifically for Skye.

Title:
Driving Single-Track Roads

Explain:

Many roads on Skye are narrow single-track roads with passing places.

Rules:

- Never park in passing places.
- Use passing places to allow vehicles to pass.
- Let faster vehicles behind you pass.
- Be cautious around blind corners.
- Expect sheep on the road.
- Drive slower than the posted maximum when necessary.
